# Demo script (draft, ~3 minutes)

Rule: the strongest moment inside the first minute; the user's real journey, main feature first; honest about limits.
Three friends from India planning Bali. The three Discord accounts are test accounts played by Maulik; say so on screen.

| Time | Screen | What we see / say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Title card | "Ever overspent on a trip because you didn't want to be the one to say it? 75% of people who travel with friends overspend." |
| 0:08–0:30 | Aarav's private channel | Aarav types *"I can do around 60k max, I'm veg, cheapest flights please."* His Treaty companion confirms in one line; he says yes; the keeper's 🔒 line appears. Caption: "Only Aarav, his own assistant and the keeper (plain code) can see this." |
| 0:30–0:40 | Quick cuts: Maulik's and Diya's channels | Same, in their own words. `#trip` shows: "✅ All 3 friends have set their limits privately." |
| 0:40–1:05 | `#trip` | *"@treaty we're going to Bali in December, first time. What should we do?"* The guide researches and registers P1, P2, P3; the keeper answers each: ✅ / ❌. Nobody's number appears anywhere. |
| 1:05–1:25 | **The wall** | Someone invites the guide into Aarav's private channel. Next check: *"⚠️ Wall breach: agent treaty-planner in Aarav's private channel. Checks are suspended."* Remove it; checks resume. Caption: "Switch room membership is the wall. The keeper checks it live." |
| 1:25–1:50 | `#trip` | Votes in plain words: "count me in for P1", "not for me", "ok fine, I'm in". Maulik: "let's lock P1". 🔒 Locked. |
| 1:50–2:15 | `#trip` | *"@treaty what can we do in Bali, and what do we need?"* Activities within the daily allowance; visa on arrival for Indian passports; documents; packing; how to get parents on board. The `trip-checklist` document. "No Google needed." |
| 2:15–2:40 | Diagram | Three kinds of agents, three kinds of rooms: your companion (your room only), the guide (#trip only), the keeper (plain code, the only bridge). `@treaty` routes per room. The keeper rebuilds from Switch's history and never writes a number down. |
| 2:40–2:52 | Evidence card | Probing: a naive bot gives away a budget in ~15 checks; the Treaty gives a coarse range and never whose. Honest: instructed AIs didn't leak in our tests, but the Treaty never even sends your number to a shared AI. |
| 2:52–3:00 | Close | "Plan together. Spend honestly. Nobody has to say it out loud." Repo link. "Three test accounts played by one person." |

## Before recording
- Keeper running with the Bali trip config; all agents online; RAM checked.
- Delete Switch's canned greetings and `!` command threads; set "Hide After Inactivity" to 1 hour.
- Fresh `trip_started_at`, so nothing from rehearsals shows up.
- Rehearse once end to end; record a backup take of the wall moment.
